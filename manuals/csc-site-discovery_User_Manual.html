3. 🔬 In-Depth Feature Guide & Workflows

CSC Overlay & Safety Checklist Enforcement (`RFI-23-001`..`011`)

Prompts field engineers to verify factory OEM BACnet cards (`Overlay, Never Gut`), hardwired VFD HOA/speed split (`RFI-23-006`), DPDT manual-reset serpentine freeze stats (`RFI-23-007`), Div 23 21 13 inline valve/well boundaries (`RFI-23-005`), and dedicated Div 26 120VAC 15A TCP circuits (`RFI-23-009`).

CLAHE Stamped-Aluminum Nameplate Enhancer & Offline Queue

Applies local canvas Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization so sun-bleached or rusted outdoor RTU/chiller data plates become legible instantly—working 100% offline in mechanical basements.

150Hz Mechanical-Room High-Pass Audio Filter

Uses WebAudio high-pass filtering to strip low-frequency fan/pump rumble (`<150Hz`) from walk-and-talk voice memos for crystal-clear Whisper transcription back at the office.
