1. ⚡ 60-Second Quickstart Guide

1
Set Your Default Project Tag (`⚙️ Settings`)
Tap `⚙️ Settings` in the top system strip to configure your default Project / Job Tag (pre-fills automatically on every new note) or type a custom tag per entry.
2
Snap a Compressed Rear-Camera Photo (`📷`)
Tap `📷 Snap / Attach Photo` to launch your Android rear camera. RollupBridge automatically resizes (`1280px` max width) and JPEG-compresses (`82%`) images so hundreds of snaps fit comfortably on device.
3
Log Observation & Save Privately (`💾`)
Enter a quick field observation and tap `💾 Save Entry`. Entries are stored strictly under your signed-in Google email namespace.
4
Export Structured CSV (`📤 Export CSV`)
Tap `📤 Export CSV` at the top right to download a clean spreadsheet of timestamps, project tags, notes, and photo flags.

3. 🔬 In-Depth Feature Guide & Workflows

Client-Side Canvas Photo Compression (`RollupBridge.camera`)

Raw 12MP–50MP phone photos (`5–12 MB` each) would quickly exhaust browser storage quotas. Field Snap compresses captures on-device into crisp `~120–220 KB` JPEGs in milliseconds.

Zero Cross-User Leakage

Two technicians signing into the same shared tablet or their own phones each see only their own `rollup::v1::u::<email>::app::field-snap::entries` bucket.
