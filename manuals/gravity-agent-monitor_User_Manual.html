1

Sign into RollupOS as Admin and launch Gravity Agent Monitor. The app automatically unlocks via your Admin session and connects to your primary LAN sidecar (`192.168.86.47:8765`) or Tailscale mesh node.

3. 🔬 In-Depth Feature Guide & Workflows

Multi-PC 'Follow-Me' Dynamic Fleet

Add both your Home PC and Office PC (via LAN IP or `100.x.y.z` Tailscale IP) under Ops & Launcher. Gravity polls all configured nodes in parallel and automatically highlights whichever workstation is actively executing turns.

Direct 2-Way AgentAPI Prompt & Skill Dispatch

Uses your workstation's authenticated `X-Gravity-Token` header to dispatch replies into an existing conversation or spin up a new chat scoped to any registered project (`FF_2026`, `Div2`, `CSC Skills`, etc.) with optional pre-populated skill prompts.

Coffee / WakeLock Mode for Unattended Builds

Tap `☕ Toggle Keep-Awake` in Ops & Launcher to hold an Android screen WakeLock while monitoring long `/goal` builds or deployments.
