3. 🔬 In-Depth Feature Guide & Workflows

100% Deterministic Nibble Mask & Checksum Engine (`Code -> Never LLM`)

Evaluates `set`, `bits`, and `offset` nibble operations across multi-step recipes with rolling same-address state compounding and recomputes exact Ford line checksums (`sum(header + data[:-1]) & 0xFF`).

3-Tier Safety Gating & EV Applicability Filtering

Automatically locks Tier 3 safety/HV/powertrain/ADAS modules (`7E7 BECM`, `746 SOBDMC`, `760 ABS`, `730 PSCM`, `706 IPMA`) and flags `ICE_ONLY` gas-engine recipes so they cannot be accidentally scheduled on your Lightning.

Ford Motorcraft `.ab` XML & FORScan `.abt` Diffing

Parses both plain-text FORScan `.abt` backups and official Ford Motorcraft `.ab` XML files directly on your phone, surfacing byte-by-byte factory drift and hardware part numbers (`F111`, `F113`, `F188`).
